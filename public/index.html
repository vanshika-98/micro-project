Culinary Artistry Meets The Arabian Sea

A Symphony of Coastal Flavors on Marine Drive

Perched gracefully along Mumbai's illustrious seaside Promenade, The Ivory Table was envisioned as an ode to maritime elegance and progressive gastronomy. Here, century-old Malabar and Konkani culinary secrets intertwine with Mediterranean sophistication.

From Arabian Sea catch delivered daily by artisanal fishermen at dawn to cold-pressed Kashmiri saffron and wild Himalayan morels, every dish is crafted with reverence. As twilight casts its violet and gold hues over the Arabian waters, our tables transform into Mumbai's most romantic dining sanctuary.
